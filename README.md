# Free Online Board & Card Game Alternatives

This repository is a GitHub-hosted markdown document of free websites where you can play board and card games online (similar to the kind of experience people look for with sites like Colonist.io).

## General Board Game Platforms

- [Board Game Arena](https://boardgamearena.com/) — Large library of modern board games, with many free-to-play titles.
- [Yucata](https://www.yucata.de/en) — Browser-based async turn system for many strategy board games.
- [Tabletopia](https://tabletopia.com/) — Digital tabletop simulator with free access to a rotating set of games.

## Catan-Like / Strategy Alternatives

- [Colonist.io](https://colonist.io/) — Popular online Catan-style platform with free public play.
- [Catan Universe](https://catanuniverse.com/) — Official Catan platform with limited free access.

## Chess & Abstract Strategy

- [Lichess](https://lichess.org/) — Fully free and open-source chess platform.
- [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/play) — Major chess platform with a generous free tier.
- [PyChess](https://www.pychess.org/) — Free browser chess with variants.

## Card Game Platforms

- [CardzMania](https://www.cardzmania.com/) — Multiplayer card games in browser (Hearts, Spades, Euchre, etc.).
- [Netgames.io](https://www.netgames.io/) — Free online social games including card games like Hearts and Oh Hell.
- [World of Card Games](https://worldofcardgames.com/) — Classic card games playable directly in browser.

## Social Deduction / Party-Style

- [Secret Hitler Online](https://secrethitler.io/) — Browser-based adaptation of Secret Hitler.
- [Pretend You’re Xyzzy](https://pretendyoure.xyz/zy/) — Free Cards Against Humanity-style online play.

## Notes

- Free features can change over time; some platforms also offer premium subscriptions.
- If you want, this list can be expanded later by category (co-op, async-only, mobile-friendly, etc.).
