
# 🎲 Top List of Free Online Board and Card Game Alternatives

Some games used to have an official online version and now don't (licensing ended, servers shut down, the app was pulled), and some never had a free one at all. This list collects the free, browser-based or open-source alternatives people use instead — in the spirit of **Colonist.io** for Catan.

> **Heads up:** Most of these are unofficial fan projects or "inspired by" clones. Sites come and go, names change to avoid trademarks, and some may add ads or optional paid tiers. Support the original designers by buying the physical game if you enjoy it.

## 🧠 Analysis Engines & Competitive Training

Tools that go beyond just playing: engine analysis, move-by-move mistake detection, puzzles and learning resources. Think **Stockfish on Lichess**, but for other games.

| Game | Tool / Platform | Link | What It Offers |
|---|---|---|---|

| Go | **KaTrain** | [github.com/sanderland/katrain](https://github.com/sanderland/katrain) | Free trainer built on the KataGo AI; shows point loss per move and teaching games. |

| Go | OGS AI Review | [online-go.com](https://online-go.com) | Automatic AI review of your games (fuller reviews on paid tiers). |
| Backgammon | **GNU Backgammon** | [gnubg.org](https://www.gnubg.org) | Free, strong analysis: rates every move and cube decision, error rates per match. |
| Backgammon | Backgammon Galaxy | [backgammongalaxy.com](https://www.backgammongalaxy.com) | Built-in post-game analysis with a performance rating. |
| Scrabble | **Woogles.io** | [woogles.io](https://woogles.io) | Built-in move analyzer, annotated games and puzzles. |

| Magic: The Gathering (Arena) | 17lands | [17lands.com](https://www.17lands.com) | Free draft stats and win-rate data from real games; widely used to improve at Limited. |

## 💡 Tips

- **No account needed:** Colonist.io, [NowPlay.gg](https://nowplay.gg) Richup.io, Skribbl.io, Gartic Phone, Horsepaste and Netgames.io all let you jump in with a room link.
- **Playing with friends remotely:** pair any of these with Discord or a video call.
- **Can't find a clone?** Check Board Game Arena first, then build it yourself on [NowPlay.gg](https://nowplay.gg).
